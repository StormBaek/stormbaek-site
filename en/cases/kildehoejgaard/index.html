The brief

A website that shows
the business behind it.

The previous website put all the company's information on one long page. We gave Kildehøjgård a new visual direction and a structure with room for services, projects and local history.

What we changed

01

A local identity

Vejby, Tisvilde and the company's work now take centre stage. The new homepage shows who Kildehøjgård are and where they work.

02

Clear services

Landscaping, gardens, nature management and construction each have a clear entry point. Visitors can find the service that fits their project.

03

Easy to get in touch

Phone and email are visible on the homepage. Mobile visitors can call directly, and the contact form collects the key details about the project.
