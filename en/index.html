Questions

What people usually ask.

Standard websites are delivered within 14 working days of kickoff, once the scope is accepted and we have your materials and access. The schedule pauses while we wait for replies or materials. Systems and web apps have individual timelines.

We build the solution ourselves. We agree on how you can edit text, images and other content as part of the project. If you need an admin panel for a system or web app, we include it in the quote.

Yes. After full payment, you receive the website source code, a guide and an overview of hosting and integrations. You can edit the code yourself or freely choose another developer or agency to continue the work. We help with the handover. Editing code directly requires technical knowledge; a content editor is agreed separately. Third-party components retain their own licences.

We help set up your domain, hosting and email as agreed for the project. You pay providers directly; these costs are not part of our project price. Ongoing maintenance and support are optional.

Our website package costs DKK 8,995 excluding VAT and includes a homepage and up to 5 additional pages. Systems and web apps receive an individual quote based on scope. You get a fixed price for the agreed solution, and extra requests are priced and approved before we work on them.

About us

Two people. Copenhagen and North Zealand.

Stormbæk is a small Danish web agency. We build websites, online stores and web solutions for businesses that depend on local customers — and we build them ourselves, from the first sketch to launch.

We can meet in person in Copenhagen or North Zealand if you prefer a handshake to an email. Otherwise, we can work together by phone and video call.

Co-owner · development
