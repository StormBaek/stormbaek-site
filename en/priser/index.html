What people usually ask

What costs extra?
You pay the provider directly for your domain, hosting and any licences — typically a few hundred Danish kroner a year. All our work within the agreed scope is covered by the fixed price. Extra requests are priced and approved before we work on them.
Does the 14-day timeline always apply?
The 14 working days apply to standard website projects and start at kickoff once we have materials and access. We pause the timeline while waiting for replies or third parties. Web apps and systems have individual timelines.
What if I want to make changes afterwards?
You can work with us, edit the source code yourself or choose another developer or agency. Source code is handed over after full payment. If you want our help, we agree on scope and price before starting.
What do I need to provide?
A 20-minute meeting, your logo if you have one, and answers to a few questions along the way. We help with copy and images.

20 minutes. Let's get acquainted.

A 20-minute conversation about your project, your goals and how we can help. We look at your current situation and give an honest assessment — no sales pitch, no obligations. Bring a brief description of what you need so we can make the most of the time.
