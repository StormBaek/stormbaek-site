How it works.

Six steps. The full picture. Know what happens — and when we need your input.

  1. 01

    We talk

    A short conversation to clarify your goals, pages, features and style.

    Tell us about your business
  2. 02

    You get a fixed price

    You get the scope, price and timeline in writing. We collect materials and access.

    You approve the scope
  3. 03

    You see your design draft

    See the direction before we build. Decline the first draft and you can stop without paying.

    You approve the design in writing
  4. 04

    Follow our progress and share feedback
  5. 05

    You approve the finished site
  6. 06

    We help with launch and handover. You receive the source code after full payment.

    You choose how to run your site

Pricing, payment and extra requests

Scope and fixed price

A fixed price for the agreed scope. Extra requests and scope changes are priced and approved in writing before we work on them. We send a written scope with the quote so it is always clear what is included — and what is not.

Payment

Pay in one instalment after launch, or spread the cost over 6 months interest-free. All prices exclude VAT. Invoicing starts only after you have approved the design draft.

Design draft, approval and cancellation

Risk-free design draft

You see your design draft before we build. Decline the draft and you pay nothing — no deposit or setup fee. Once you approve the draft in writing, we enter the build phase and the fixed-price agreement becomes binding. The option to cancel without payment applies once, at the first design draft, before the build phase.

Feedback and approvals

You approve the design draft and the finished solution in writing (email is sufficient). We ask for a reply within 5 working days. If we do not hear from you, we pause the timeline until we do.

Cancellation

Before approving the design draft, you may stop at no cost. After approval, the fixed price for the agreed scope is binding. If the project stalls due to missing materials or replies for more than 60 days, we may close it and invoice for work completed.

Materials, access and timeline

Materials and access

We typically need your logo, images, any copy, and access to your domain, hosting and relevant accounts. The website package includes help with copy and images. We agree the materials and scope before starting. The project cannot start without materials and access.

Delivery time

14 working days for a standard website project. The timeline starts at kickoff once the scope is accepted and we have the necessary materials and access. We pause it while waiting for replies, content or third parties. Web apps and system projects have individual timelines.

Ownership, hosting and licences

Ownership

Once the project is fully paid, you own the design, content and finished solution. You receive the source code, a guide and an overview of hosting and integrations so another developer can continue the work. We help with the handover; by agreement, the code can be delivered to your own GitHub repository. Standard components, libraries and third-party tools retain their own licences.

Domain, hosting and third parties

You pay providers directly for your domain, hosting, licences and payment solutions; these are not included in the fixed price. We outline the expected costs before starting and set everything up for you.

Bug fixes and ongoing help

Bug fixes after launch

We fix bugs in the delivered solution free of charge for 30 days after launch. New requests and additions are not bugs — we agree a fixed price before starting them.

Ongoing maintenance

Maintenance, updates, support and further development are optional, at a fixed monthly price. You can always choose to manage the website yourself.

Case studies, testimonials and referrals

Case study and testimonial

We only showcase your project as a case study or use a testimonial if you have agreed in writing. This is agreed separately from the project itself.

Referral programme

If you refer a business that becomes a paying Stormbæk client, you receive a refund. The refund is 50% of the price you paid for the website itself, up to DKK 8,995. Add-ons, hosting, maintenance, subscriptions and other services are not included in the calculation. Conditions: one refund per original client; the referred business must be completely new to Stormbæk; they must agree to the introduction before we contact them; the refund is paid only once the new client's project is fully paid; self-referrals and businesses with the same owner or beneficial owners are excluded; you must tell the referred business that you receive a financial benefit from the referral.

These terms are a plain-language summary. The specific project is always agreed in a written quote and contract, which take precedence in the event of a disagreement. Questions? Email info@stormbaek.com.
