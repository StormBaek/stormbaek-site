What does a professional website cost in 2026?

The price depends on the scope. Start with our DKK 8,995 package excl. VAT, then learn how to compare quotes without overlooking extras and ongoing costs.

Updated 5 October 2026

What does DKK 8,995 include?

Stormbæk’s website package costs DKK 8,995 excl. VAT. It includes bespoke design, a homepage and up to 5 subpages, responsive design, a contact form and booking link, help with copy and images, and basic technical SEO.

What can change the price?

More pages, custom integrations, payments and user accounts can change the scope. A booking link is already included; a custom booking system is a different task. Any additions are quoted and approved before work begins.

What are the ongoing costs?

Domain, hosting and any third-party licences are paid separately. The amount depends on the agreed setup. Ongoing SEO, AI visibility, advertising and later changes are optional. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google in addition to our management fee.

Compare the scope, not just the headline price

Ask for a written scope covering pages, features, content, revisions and delivery. Also ask who owns the code, what hosting costs and how to change providers. A higher price does not guarantee better results, and a lower price does not rule out bespoke design.

You receive the source code

After full payment, you receive the source code. You can work on it yourself or choose another developer or agency. Third-party services and licences have their own terms.

When is the website ready, and when do you pay?

Standard website projects have a 14-working-day schedule from kickoff once materials and access are ready. Waiting for feedback or third parties pauses the schedule. Systems have individual timelines. Payment can be made in full or in interest-free instalments over 6 months. Rejecting the first design draft costs nothing; written approval starts the binding build agreement.
