Guide · Pricing 2026

How much does a professional website cost in 2026?

The short answer: between DKK 10,000 and DKK 200,000+. The longer answer depends on who you hire and what the website needs to do. Here is a transparent breakdown without the sales fluff — so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Price ranges in the Danish market

DIY tools (Wix, Squarespace)

DKK 0–5,000
  • Low starting cost
  • Quick to get started
  • Template look — hard to stand out
  • Ongoing subscription plus charges for most extras
  • No strategy or optimisation

Freelancer

DKK 10,000–40,000
  • Personal contact
  • Flexible pricing
  • Quality varies considerably
  • Often just one person — vulnerable to illness or busy periods
  • SEO, copy and design rarely included together

Professional web agency

DKK 40,000–150,000
  • Strategy, design and development together
  • Optimised for leads and sales
  • Support and further development after launch
  • Higher initial investment
  • Requires a clear brief from you

Enterprise agency

DKK 200,000+
  • Tailored to large organisations
  • A full project team
  • Overkill for small and medium-sized businesses
  • Long delivery times (3–6 months)

What drives the price?

Two websites at the same price can deliver very different results. Value is not determined by the number of pages — it is about how well the website turns visitors into customers. These are the factors that actually affect the price:

Number of pages

A landing page costs less than a website with 20 pages, a blog and a customer portal.

Bespoke design vs. a template

A bespoke visual identity costs more — but sets you apart from competitors.

Content and copy

Will the agency write the copy, or will you provide it? Writing takes time.

Integrations

Booking, payments, CRM, email and member areas — each integration adds cost.

SEO and performance

Technical SEO, speed and structured data should be built in from the start, not added later.

Support after launch

Backups, updates and small adjustments — always ask what is included.

What makes sense for your business?

Are you self-employed or just starting out? Start with a focused one-page website from a freelancer or small agency. Focus on one clear offer and one strong call to action.

Do you run an established business with customers? Then your website is more than a business card — it is your most important sales tool. It can make sense to invest DKK 40,000–100,000 in something that actively generates leads.

Are you growing quickly or looking to scale? Then your website needs to support future growth — a CMS, integrations and SEO foundations. Expect DKK 80,000–150,000.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get a good website for less than DKK 20,000?

Yes — if it is small, focused and built by a skilled freelancer. But do not expect bespoke design, in-depth SEO or ongoing support.

At Stormbæk, we deliver most websites within 14 working days of kickoff, once materials and access are in place. Larger projects with integrations and customer portals take 4–8 weeks.

Are there ongoing costs?

Yes — domain (around DKK 100/year), hosting (around DKK 50–500/month), and possibly a support subscription. We are always clear about recurring costs.
