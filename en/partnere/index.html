A paid pilot. A concrete project.

Start with one landing page or a small business website. We agree the deliverables in writing before work starts. No ongoing partnership commitment is needed to try working with us.

  1. 01

    Scope and materials

    You send designs or references, page content and any technical requirements. We clarify whether the project is a good fit and who supplies what.

  2. 02

    Price and deadline

    You receive a fixed price for the agreed scope. Page count, feedback rounds, integrations and delivery are agreed before work starts. Additional work requires a new agreement.

  3. 03

    Review and handover

    You review the website before it goes live. After full payment, we hand over the source code and a guide. Hosting and ongoing maintenance are agreed separately.
